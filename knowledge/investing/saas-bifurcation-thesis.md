# SaaS Bifurcation Thesis — Fortress vs. Commodity

**Date:** 2026-03-23
**Status:** Active thesis

## Core Insight

The market is pricing all software at the same AI-disruption discount. This is wrong. There are two categories:

### Deterministic Systems (Fortress Software)
ERP, manufacturing execution, financial compliance, healthcare records, trading platforms.

- Require 100% consistency — "correct 6/10 times" is a regulatory violation
- Massive switching costs, decades of proprietary data, embedded workflows
- AI agents can't replace them or understand their historical context
- On-premises ERP still holds **72% market share** in 2025
- Examples: SAP, Oracle ERP, Epic (healthcare), FIS/Fiserv (trading)

### Probabilistic Systems (Commodity Software)
Marketing automation, basic CRM, content tools, analytics dashboards.

- Exactly where AI excels — good enough is good enough
- Getting rapidly disrupted and likely destroyed
- Gartner: **35% of SaaS point tools replaced by AI agents by 2030**
- Examples: Notion-wrapper startups, basic analytics, content generation tools

## Market Context

- ~$2T in SaaS market cap wiped out in early 2026
- Median SaaS valuations: 18-19x EV/Revenue (pandemic peak) → ~5x today
- Apollo Global slashed software exposure in private credit from 20% → 10%
- Market treating all software as one category — that's the mistake

## Playbook for Fortress Software Companies

### Defense
1. **System of Record → System of Context** — own the AI insight layer, don't let orchestration platforms extract your data's value
2. **Per-seat → outcome-based pricing** — when AI compresses headcount, revenue should go UP not down
3. **First-class agent integration** — become the node AI agents depend on
4. **Acquire distressed competitors** at depressed multiples — buy the customer relationship, not the code
5. **Double down on compliance moats** — AI-native competitors can't replicate decades of domain expertise

### Growth
1. **AI-powered downmarket expansion** — $50K enterprise tool serves SMBs via AI self-service (potential 10x TAM)
2. **Absorb dying point solutions** — every commodity SaaS tool AI kills is a workflow your platform can claim
3. **Private cloud + perpetual license hybrid** — serve customers fleeing SaaS subscription fatigue

## Investment Implications

- Fortress software at ~5x EV/Revenue is a once-in-a-cycle buying opportunity
- Distinguish between companies with switching costs + compliance moats vs. commodity tools
- Watch for fortress companies executing the "System of Context" pivot
- Short/avoid pure-play probabilistic SaaS with no moat
