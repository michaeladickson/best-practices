- **OpenAI’s new Privacy Filter runs on your laptop so PII never hits the cloud** (The New Stack) — relevance 9/10
  OpenAI has released Privacy Filter, an open-weight, local token-classification model designed to detect and redact personally identifiable information (PII) from text before it leaves a user's device. It offers enhanced context-awareness compared to traditional methods like RegEx and supports various PII types.
  Why: Handling sensitive financial data (payroll, invoices, client info) requires robust PII protection. A local PII redaction tool like OpenAI's Privacy Filter offers a critical layer of security and compliance before data is processed by cloud-based LLMs like Gemini.
